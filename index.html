About Me

Umair Riaz - Sr. Fullstack Developer

My name is Umair Riaz, and I am a passionate and experienced Full Stack Developer, with a career that began in 2018. I started my journey as a freelance WordPress developer, gradually evolving into a full-stack professional through hands-on experience with both startups and established organizations.

Over the years, I’ve built a strong foundation in WordPress development, working extensively on custom themes, plugins, and complete WooCommerce solutions. My expertise includes:

  • Custom plugin development & theme customization
  • WooCommerce store development with tailored Add to Cart, Checkout, and Payment Gateway Integrations (PayPal, Stripe, and custom gateways)
  • Website speed optimization and security hardening
  • REST API development and third-party API integration in WordPress

I’m a solution-driven developer who loves turning complex challenges into clean, scalable, and high-performance web applications tailored for real-world business needs.

Full-Stack Development Expertise

In addition to my extensive WordPress experience, I have hands-on experience in developing complete multi-vendor platforms with Laravel, implementing essential modules such as User, Admin, and Vendor authentication and authorization, along with comprehensive product, cart, coupon, and checkout management systems. Additionally, I’ve built and maintained campaign management features and wallet systems to support complex eCommerce operations.

On the frontend, I specialize in creating modern, responsive user interfaces using React.js combined with Redux Toolkit and Tailwind CSS. I also work proficiently with Vue.js, Bootstrap, jQuery, HTML5, and CSS3 to deliver seamless and user-friendly designs across different platforms and devices.

In the eCommerce space, I’ve built and customized Shopify stores from the ground up, including custom theme development and app integration. I also bring solid experience with Magento, delivering tailored solutions for scalable online stores.

My backend expertise includes PHP with Object-Oriented Programming (OOP), Laravel, and CodeIgniter, along with JavaScript-based stacks like Node.js and Express.js, enabling me to build full-stack solutions that are efficient, scalable, and secure.
